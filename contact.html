Before you call

Short answers

How often can you come?

Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or once. You set the schedule. A one-time clean is fine for a move or an event.

What if I need to reschedule?

Call at least 48 hours ahead when you can, on 0309-2914489.

What if the job needs to change?

Say what is different. If a new quote is needed, you get it before the extra work is done.

How do I pay?

Payment is due on the day of the visit. Cash or cheque made out to HWM. You receive a receipt.

Who do I tell if something was missed?

Tell the crew while they are on site, or call 0309-2914489. The company asks you to flag a cleaning problem within 24 hours.
