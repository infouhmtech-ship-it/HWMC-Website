Services

Work matched to the site, not a fixed package.

Visits can be daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or a single clean. Reschedule with at least 48 hours’ notice when you can. If the scope changes, we requote before doing the extra work.

01

Door-to-door waste collection

Waste is picked up from homes and shops on an agreed round. Food waste, paper, bottles, and ordinary household rubbish are all part of a normal collection. Say if a visit needs to include something heavier, such as steel or a clear-out.

02

Single-point collection

Societies, plazas, and factories often do better with one staffed or scheduled point instead of a door-to-door round. We lift from that point and move the load in company vehicles. Waste can be held in the company warehouse before it goes on.

03

Janitorial and building care

Cleaning for commercial and residential buildings: public areas, glass, floors, and stewarding. Crews are trained for the job, wear uniform, and carry a service card. Cleaning products used on these jobs are chosen to avoid leaving a harsh residue.

04

One-time and scheduled cleaning

Book a single visit for a move, an event, or a site that needs catching up. Most clients stay on a weekly or bi-weekly round; a monthly round is also available. You set the frequency.

05

Garbage bags and dustbins

Bags make wet food waste easier to lift and easier to keep sealed. We supply bags to clients so collection day is simpler, and we place dustbins where a site needs people to have somewhere to throw waste in the first place.

06

Scrap buying

Plastic scrap, plastic dana, paper scrap, and rubber scrap can be sold to us for cash rather than sent out as rubbish. Tell us the material and the approximate quantity when you call, and we will say whether that load is one we take.

On the day

What you should expect from a visit.

  • A written agreement for ongoing work.
  • Staff in uniform, with a service card.
  • Transport in the company’s waste vehicles.
  • Payment on the day, by cash or cheque to HWM, and a receipt.
  • A complaint line on 0309-2914489 if something on the visit is wrong. Tell the crew while they are there, or call and it will be taken up with them.

If plans change

Call before the visit.

Give 48 hours’ notice when you need to move a booking. If you want a different frequency or extra rooms, we alter the agenda and send a new quote before that work is done.
