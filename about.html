About the company

A Lahore waste and cleaning firm, started in 2014.

Hygienic Waste Management collects waste, lifts it from housing societies, and cleans the places around it: public areas, glass, and floors, plus stewarding where a site needs it. The head office is in Garden Town. A sub office sits on Adyala Road in Rawalpindi.

The aim is a working system for collection and cleaning, with government bodies, private clients, and residents, so waste is handled properly and the places people use stay fit to be in.

Sarfraz Ahmed, Chief Executive

How the work is run

Trained crews, a written agreement, and a place to hold the waste.

Staff are trained for the cleaning and collection they are sent to do. They attend in uniform with a service card. Ongoing clients get a written agreement. Vehicles are set up to move waste, and the company keeps a warehouse for temporary holding. The work follows the rules of the Punjab environmental authority and the Punjab Health Department.

What the company holds itself to

Three working rules, from the company profile.

Do the job properly

Quality is treated as the standard of the visit, not a slogan on the van. If a clean is not right, call within a day and say what was missed.

Stay with the client’s brief

Frequency, rooms, and collection points are set with you. A change is quoted before it is done.

Look after the crew

The stated aim is a safe workplace and a team that is paid and spoken to with ordinary respect. That is how a round stays reliable.
