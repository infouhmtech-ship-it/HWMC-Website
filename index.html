How a job is booked

From the call to the next visit.

  1. 1. You describe the site

    Call, WhatsApp, or use the form. Say what needs collecting or cleaning, and how often.

  2. 2. Scope is confirmed

    We agree the place, the timing, and the price before work starts. A requote is given if the job changes.

  3. 3. The crew attends

    Waste goes in company vehicles. Cleaners follow the checklist for that site.

  4. 4. You get a receipt

    Payment is due on the day of service. Cash or cheque to HWM, with a receipt after the visit.
